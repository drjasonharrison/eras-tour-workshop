The Eras Tour of Computer Vision

Rules. Neural Nets. Vision-Language Models. One tour, three eras, no two nights the same.

Welcome to the tour. Picture three completely different eras of computer vision, each with its own sound, its own style, its own idea of what counts as "seeing." In the next two hours you're going to run all three of them, back to back, on the exact same set of photos, and watch them agree, disagree, and occasionally embarrass each other.

No laptop setup required beyond a browser. No prior computer vision background needed. Just click the badges below when it's your turn.

The three eras

Era 1: The Rules Era

Hand-crafted color and shape logic. Already built and running — you'll tweak two numbers and watch a detector succeed, then break, then succeed differently.

Era 2: The Neural Nets Era

A pretrained image classifier, no training required. One knob to turn: which labels count as "mug-like." Watch precision and recall trade off in real time.

Era 3: The Vision-Language Era

No notebook this time — just your phone and a free chat assistant. This is the only era that can tell you why it thinks what it thinks.
